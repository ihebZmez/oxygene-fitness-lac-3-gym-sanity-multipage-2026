# 11 — Client Reference

A quick guide to what your website can do and how to use it.

---

## 🎯 What the Site Does

A modern website that turns visitors into members.
Every section pushes toward a **trial session**, a **WhatsApp message**, or a **call**.

---

## 🧩 Every Section & How You Control It

### 1. Site Settings

**What**: Your club name, phone, WhatsApp number, email, address, hours, social links.
**How to use**: Change your phone number or hours once → updated everywhere on the site.
**Example**: Update Sunday hours from 08:00–14:00 to 09:00–15:00.

---

### 2. Actualités (News)

**What**: Short announcements — new classes, events, coach updates.
**How to use**: Create a new "News" in Sanity → fill title, coach, day, time, upload the poster → Publish.
**Example**:

- Title: **Zumba avec Olfa**
- Coach: Olfa
- Day: Lundi
- Time: 17:45
- Icons: 💃 🔥 💪
- CTA: Réservez votre place
- Image: your poster design

Appears on the home page instantly.

---

### 3. Promotions

**What**: Time-limited offers with auto-expiry.
**How to use**: Set the offer, discount, end date, image → Publish. It hides itself after the end date.
**Example**:

- Title: **NEW MEMBER OFFER**
- Discount: **1 mois + 1 semaine GRATUITE**
- End date: 31/12/2026
- Highlight: ON

---

### 4. Activities

**What**: Your class catalogue (Zumba, Boxing, Yoga…).
**How to use**: Add name, description, image, icon, level, duration → Publish.
**Example**: Add "Pilates — Reformer" with image and price.

---

### 5. Coaches

**What**: Your team profiles.
**How to use**: Add name, role, photo, specialties, years of experience.
**Example**: New coach "Olfa" → appears in the coaches grid.

---

### 6. Personal Training

**What**: Your 1-on-1 premium offer.
**How to use**: Edit the pillars, packages, prices, photo → Publish.
**Example**: Change the "Transformation" pack from 600 DT to 550 DT for a promo.

---

### 7. Schedule (Planning)

**What**: Weekly class schedule.
**How to use**: Add a class per day with time, activity, coach, room.
**Example**: Add "Zumba — Lundi 17:45 — Olfa — Studio 2".

---

### 8. Pricing

**What**: Membership plans.
**How to use**: Edit name, price, features, popular toggle.
**Example**: Change Pro plan from 149 DT to 159 DT.

---

### 9. Corporate

**What**: Your B2B offer for companies.
**How to use**: Edit benefits, services, image, contact email.
**Example**: Add "Team building sessions on Fridays".

---

### 10. Shop & Partners

**What**: Recommended products (protein, gloves, shoes…).
**How to use**: Add name, brand, price, image, CTA (WhatsApp or partner link).
**Example**: Add "Whey Isolate — 189 DT — Commander via WhatsApp".

---

### 11. Testimonials

**What**: Short member quotes.
**How to use**: Add name, role, quote, rating, photo.
**Example**: "Ines — Coaching personnel — 5★ — ...".

---

### 12. Transformations

**What**: Before / After results (with consent).
**How to use**: Upload both photos, add duration, weight loss, quote → publish only if consent given.
**Example**:

- Name: Mehdi B.
- Duration: 4 mois
- Weight loss: 12 kg
- Quote: "After 4 months..."

---

## ✍️ How to Edit Content (Every Day)

1. Go to the Sanity Studio URL assigned to this project
2. Log in with your email
3. Choose a section (Actualités, Promotions, Coaches…)
4. Click **Create new** or click an existing item
5. Fill in the fields, upload images
6. Click **Publish**
7. Refresh your website — the change is live

---

## 🚦 Rules of Thumb

| Action                           | How                       |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Hide an item without deleting    | Turn **Active** OFF       |
| Show an item again               | Turn **Active** ON        |
| Offer that expires automatically | Fill the **End date**     |
| Highlight an offer               | Turn **Highlight** ON     |
| Publish a transformation         | Only if **Consent** is ON |

---

## 📸 Image Guidelines

- **Photos**: JPG/PNG, at least 1200 px wide
- **Posters**: 1080×1350 (Instagram portrait ratio)
- **Product photos**: square, clean white or dark background
- **Before/After**: same lighting, same pose, same framing
- **Logo**: transparent PNG

---

## 🔒 What Stays Private

You can edit everything except:

- Site structure
- Users & permissions
- API keys / technical settings

Those stay with the developer.
