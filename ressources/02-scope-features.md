# 02 — Scope & Features

## Public Pages

- **Home** — hero, promotions, about, activities, coaches, personal training, schedule preview, pricing, testimonials, transformations, CTA
- **Activités** — full activity catalogue
- **Tarifs** — pricing plans + offers
- **Coaching Personnel** — 1-on-1 packages
- **Planning** — weekly class schedule
- **Corporate** — B2B wellness offer
- **Actualités** — news & announcements
- **Shop** — partner products with WhatsApp CTA
- **Contact** — reservation form, map, club info

## Conversion Features

- Trial-session form
- WhatsApp floating button (contextual messages)
- Phone / email / social links
- Repeated CTAs on every page
- Promotion expiry auto-handled

## CMS (Sanity)

Manage: news · pricing · activities · schedule · coaches · testimonials · transformations · corporate offers · partner products · personal training · site settings.

## Limits

- No payment / member accounts
- EmailJS credentials to be configured
- Arabic prepared but not loaded
